## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** Riverty's First Consumer Card
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Nothing collapses in 12 months, but Riverty's position weakens slowly.-
1. Klarna pulls further ahead
2. Heavy users and interest income go elsewhere
3. The card slips further
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Reaching for Klarna or PayPal, which are already on her phone. The brief says she uses whichever option appears at checkout and rarely goes looking for Riverty. So the behaviour isn't a workaround — it's indifference. That's worse than friction, because friction implies she wanted you and couldn't get there.
Paying in-store with a normal debit or credit card. Every physical purchase is a transaction Riverty never sees, and it's most of her spending.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Riverty's most loyal users carry Klarna and PayPal for everything outside its merchant base — and every month without a card is a month those habits harden around someone else's brand, in the market where the gap is already widest.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** A Riverty card allows customers "keep it first, pay later" anywhere, in stores and beyond partner merchants, and gives Riverty a direct consumer relationship.
Why launch now:
Get exemption from CCD2 regulations
Klarna is already ahead in Germany and builds card habits every month.
The Mastercard and Paymentology platform is already paid for.
The Amazon Business pilot (October 2026) is absorbing capacity, so without a committed slot the card slips.
