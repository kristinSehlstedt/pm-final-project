# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** A heavy BNPL user (8 purchases a month) and Riverty Flex user who also carries Klarna and PayPal and falls back to her debit cards everywhere else.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** Persona uses card 5 times per month
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** Moment of Misery is the till. Online she uses pay later on almost every order, but in a shop she has no way to decide later and pay afterwards from home.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Number of  successful purchase

## Scan the backlog & set a human baseline
- **My instinctive “quick wins” before touching the AI (2 to 3 feature IDs + why):** Pay later at any card terminal: decide after the purchase, as she does online. This is the bet, and it needs a feasibility check.
Instant Apple Pay and Google Pay provisioning: without it, the card is "dead on arrival".
Soft eligibility pre-check: cuts credit-check anxiety before a formal application.
Fixed, automatic monthly repayment: predictable cost, no rolling debt.
Self-set spending cap: she controls her limit and the overspending fear.
Total-cost summary before each plan: full monthly cost up front, no catch in the terms.
One repayment view, online and in-store: a single place to see what she owes.
In-app returns with refund tracking: she sees when her money comes back.
Selective cashback or 0% offers: a reason to replace a card already in her wallet.
Merchant dashboard with sales-lift reporting: shows merchants whether the card adds sales.
